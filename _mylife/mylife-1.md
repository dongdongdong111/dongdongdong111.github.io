VALORANT is a free-to-play, 5v5 tactical first-person shooter developed and published by Riot Games. In the game, two teams compete against each other, with one team attacking and the other defending. Players choose agents with unique abilities and work together using strategy, teamwork, and precise shooting skills to win rounds. The main objective is to plant or defuse the Spike. VALORANT combines tactical gunplay with character-based abilities, making it a competitive game that requires both individual skill and teamwork.
